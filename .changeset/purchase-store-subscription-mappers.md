`mapSubscription` for the App Store and Google Play, so a store's own states read the same as Stripe's in the shared `SubscriptionState` (`{ status, autoRenewEnabled, trialStart, trialEnd }`, exported from the root; Stripe's `StripeSubscriptionState` extends it). `@shware/purchase/google-play`: `mapSubscription(purchase)` replaces `mapSubscriptionStatus(state)` and reads the whole `purchases.subscriptionsv2` resource: `SUBSCRIPTION_STATE_CANCELED` is auto-renew off while the paid period runs, so it is `ACTIVE` (or `TRIALING`) with `autoRenewEnabled: false` and `EXPIRED` once every line item's `expiryTime` has passed, instead of the terminal `CANCELED` that dropped the customer's entitlement; a purchase with `replacementCancellation` is `REPLACED`; a line item in its `offerPhase.freeTrial` makes the subscription `TRIALING` with `trialStart` (`startTime`) and `trialEnd` (`expiryTime`). `@shware/purchase/app-store`: `mapSubscription({ status, transaction, renewalInfo })` maps Get All Subscription Statuses (`ACTIVE`, `EXPIRED`, `BILLING_RETRY`, `IN_GRACE_PERIOD`, revoked → `CANCELED`), `TRIALING` with the transaction's dates while the latest transaction is a `FREE_TRIAL` offer, and `autoRenewEnabled` from `autoRenewStatus`, off once the subscription is over. The `TRIALING`, `CANCELED`, `REPLACED` and `EXPIRED` docs now say what each platform reports; `TRIALING` is produced by all three mappers.
