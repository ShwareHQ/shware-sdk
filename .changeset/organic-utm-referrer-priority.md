`classifyTouch`: a utm, click id or ad landing page whose touch lands in an organic channel group, referral or email (`utm_source=chatgpt.com`, `utm_medium=organic`, a newsletter) now ranks with the referrer (`TOUCH_PRIORITY.referrer`) instead of as a campaign, so it no longer takes the credit from an ad clicked earlier in the attribution window. When a product rule matched too, the stronger of the two tiers holds (a referral link shared under a social utm keeps the programme's tier). Reclassify stored sessions after upgrading.
