The studio keeps one design and drops every compatibility path: it reads only `export default defineConfig({...})` from `@shware/workflow` in workflow.config.ts (or `--config`) and compiles it with `compileConfig` — the studio's own `defineConfig`, `WorkflowUIConfig`, `EmailSettings`, the convention-based discovery (`src/workflows/`, `src/emails/index.ts`, `src/pushes/index.ts`, `src/slack/index.ts`, `src/discord/index.ts`), the action-manifest shim and the config-borne `stats` are gone, and a missing config, a default export that is not `defineConfig({...})`, a registry entry that is not an `emailTemplate` / `pushTemplate` (named by key) or a registry not exported from a module of its own is a clear error in place of the views. Registries hold only template definitions: the legacy email/push modules (`LegacyEmailModule`, `LegacyPushModule`, `EmailModule`, `PushModule`), their envelope fields (to, cc, bcc, preheader, headers), verbatim `{prop}` push rendering, `name`-based labels and the unavailable-template handling are removed, and so are the Slack/Discord registries and previews (`ChatModule`, the chat preview) — messages on those channels still appear on the canvas, without a preview, and the template list shows email and push only. The address book becomes a read-only view of `emails.from` / `replyTo` (`ResolvedStudioConfig.addresses`, `slack`, `discord` and `unavailable` are removed). Stats stay in the optional `workflow.stats.ts` (`defineStats`).
